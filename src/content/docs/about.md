title: About this guide
---

This guide was started because "AI in documentation" is a big subject.
Many technical writers, and other people adjacent to documentation, discuss AI on a regular basis.
It takes time and effort to stay up-to-date, so I'm hoping that an online collaborative resource can ease the difficulty.

## Who it's for

1. Newcomers to technical writing and documentation, whether or not they are new to AI.
2. More experienced technical writers who want to understand AI better.
3. Anybody else in the software field who works with documentation.

This guide assumes that you, the reader, can already use a computer: you can browse the Web, install programs, and use the command line for basic tasks.
